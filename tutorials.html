Use your iPad with Ableton Live

Record every iPad track in Live 12.4 over Link Audio, each as its own input and without an audio interface. With Live’s tracks back on the iPad and MIDI in both directions.

Sync external gear to Ableton Link

Send MIDI clock from Link to drum machines, grooveboxes and synths on your iPad, iPhone or Mac, set the offset and record the gear in Live.
