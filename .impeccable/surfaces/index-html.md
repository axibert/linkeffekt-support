Scope: homepage, Persuade. Audience: musicians with iPad, iPhone, Ableton Live and hardware. Action: App Store download.

## Direction contract

THESIS: abstract, minimal, techno. The page is black and white only and shows the app at once. Refuses the skeuomorphic patch bay with cables (rejected by Alex 09.11.2026) and any busy motion.

OWN-WORLD: pure black and pure white grounds alternating by section, Archivo 800 for headlines, no accent colour; green and orange appear only inside real screenshots. Routes are a pin matrix (From rows, To columns, large dot at the crossing). Beat is two tracks of numbered pads tied by a white line.

STORY: the visitor sees an app on iPad and iPhone, reads one line, picks a setup in the matrix, sees what on the beat and one bar later mean, downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: two-line headline "Link Audio and MIDI everywhere." across the full width, below it left the sentence, app icon, App Store badge, right a large iPad with an iPhone in front, cropped by the section edge.
